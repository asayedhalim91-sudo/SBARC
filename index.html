    About the data

    What the figures mean and where they come from.

    Definitions

    Budget (BAC)
    Budgeted total cost, or budgeted labour units for man-hours.
    Planned value
    The share of budget scheduled to be done by the data date.
    Earned value
    The share of budget actually done, from performance % complete.
    Progress
    Planned or earned value ÷ budget, weighted by cost or by man-hours.
    Schedule variance
    Earned − planned value. Negative means behind plan.
    SPI
    Earned ÷ planned value. Below 1.00 is behind plan; 0.98 and above shows green, below 0.90 red.
    This week
    The change since the previous week's cumulative values in the export.
    Activity status
    Complete at 100% performance; behind plan when schedule % complete exceeds performance % complete.

    Areas and structure

    • Buildings: the 13 community buildings, from the Admin Building to the Utility Building.
    • Villas: 104 villas in clusters R1 to R6, plus XV and XY.
    • Site-wide: the export's “No VILLA NO.” group, covering infrastructure, roads and networks, general requirements, testing, approvals and close-out.
    • Work packages follow the WBS order of the schedule, from Substructure through to Handing Over.

    Source

    All figures come from ahmed_sayed_sheet.xlsx, an earned-value export of the SBARC project schedule with 23,610 activities. Totals are summed from the activity rows. Cost matches the export's totals; man-hour totals can differ from its rounded summary rows by under 0.1%.

    Amounts are in the export's currency. The export does not state a data date, so the site refers to the current reporting week.
